# PROBLEM STATEMENT MAPPING

MatriVaani addresses the urgent need for vernacular pedagogy and translation among low-resource Indian tribal languages.

1. **Mother-tongue learning**: The application bridges the gap between Hindi-speaking educators and tribal students.
2. **Hindi <-> Santali translation**: Demonstrated working integration with IndicTrans2 to support Santali education.
3. **Ho speech recognition**: Incorporated a state-of-the-art Wav2Vec2 model fine-tuned specifically for Ho, capable of transcribing Ho speech into the Warang Citi/Devanagari script.
4. **Classroom interaction**: Features a PTT (Push-To-Talk) Classroom screen tailored to live transcriptions and translations.
5. **Teacher-oriented workflow**: Teachers can record their lectures, view transcripts, and broadcast translated text dynamically.
6. **Audio support**: TTS integration is actively supported via Sarvam for Hindi/Santali, promoting accessibility.
7. **Offline/local processing**: The core Ho ASR operates entirely offline without an internet connection, securing privacy and demonstrating offline AI capability.
8. **Online provider integration**: Seamlessly routes high-resource demands (e.g. IndicTrans2) to online and WSL-hosted solutions.
9. **Low-resource language focus**: Actively combats the digital extinction of Ho and Santali.
10. **Current limitations**: Ho-to-Hindi translation models and Ho TTS are pending future development and are securely handled by the UI currently.
