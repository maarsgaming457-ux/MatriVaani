## 2. Evaluation Criteria (Planned)
When a model is successfully trained, native Santhali speakers will evaluate the generated audio against the following criteria:
1. **Pronunciation**: Are the Ol Chiki phonemes pronounced correctly?
2. **Intelligibility**: Can the listener clearly understand the words?
3. **Naturalness**: Does the speech sound robotic or human-like?
4. **Rhythm/Prosody**: Are the pauses and intonations appropriate for educational contexts?
5. **Educational Terminology**: Are complex classroom terms articulated clearly?
